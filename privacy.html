Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Short version

Your photos and PDFs stay on your device. We do not upload them, we do not store them, and we do not sell data.

2. What happens to your files

The browser reads the files you choose. Conversion and compression happen in your browser's memory. The finished file is saved by your browser to your device. Nothing is sent to our servers.

3. Information we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses or passwords.
  • No advertising or tracking scripts.
  • No cookies used to identify you.

4. Technical data

The PDF libraries are served from this website itself, so the page does not load anything from other servers. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability. We do not link these logs to your files.

5. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Your choices

Since we store nothing, you control your data. Delete a PDF from your Downloads folder whenever you like, and clear your browser history to remove local traces.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: please use the Contact us form.
