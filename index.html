Works on

iPhone and iPad: Safari 15 or newer. HEIC photos from the camera are supported.

Android: Chrome, Samsung Internet or Firefox. JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF are supported. For HEIC photos, set the camera format to JPG in Settings, or convert them first.
